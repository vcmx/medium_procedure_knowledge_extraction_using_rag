## perkins

This Perkins workshop manual provides comprehensive service and repair information for various 4.236 series diesel engines, including detailed technical data, fault diagnosis, and procedures for maintaining and assembling components such as the cylinder head, pistons, connecting rods, crankshaft, lubricating system, cooling system, and turbocharger. It also includes a marine supplement covering specific components and maintenance for marine engine applications.
